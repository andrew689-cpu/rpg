# Map: Mendvale

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A complete game-design + technical spec for **Mendvale**, an original 2D pixel-art Creature-collecting RPG for Android and iOS, released free as a hobby project. It must be detailed enough to hand to agents for slicing into implementation tickets, with no design decisions left open.

## Notes

- **Domain:** game design + mobile game engineering. Original IP only: no Pokémon names, creatures, sprites, music or look. Genre mechanics (catching, turn-based battles, types, evolution, parties) are fair game.
- **Scale:** target **Medium** (1 region, ~100 Creatures, 15–25h, ~8 boss challenges), but every system must be designed so the game can **grow to Large** (multiple regions, 150+ Creatures) without rewrites: content is data, not code.
- **Platforms:** Android and iOS. Offline single-player first.
- **Builder:** mostly Claude agents, with the human reviewing. Specs should break into small, independently testable modules.
- **Art budget:** $0 for now (CC0 packs, self-drawn, generated placeholders). Art must be swappable without code changes. Revisit budget later.
- **Vocabulary:** see `GLOSSARY.md` at the repo root. Specs and code use the generic glossary terms (Creature, Trainer, Lure, Sanctuary, Collection log); the in-world names (Verdling, Mender, Offering, Haven, Almanac) are display strings only.
- **Names:** the title and codename are both **Mendvale** (`io.github.andrew689cpu.mendvale`). The GitHub repo stays `rpg`. The effort lives in `.scratch/mendvale/`, and the local repo folder is `mendvale` (see [What are the game's title and the in-world name for Creatures?](issues/06-naming-and-branding.md)).
- **Skills every session should consult:** `grilling` + `domain-modeling` for grilling tickets; `prototype` for prototype tickets; `research` for research tickets; `codebase-design` for architecture tickets.
- **Tracker:** local markdown (see `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`). Tickets live in `issues/`; research findings live on `research/<name>` branches, pushed to `origin` (github.com/andrew689-cpu/rpg) and never merged.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per resolved ticket: [title](issues/NN-slug.md): gist -->

- [What do Google Play and the Apple App Store require for a free hobby game?](issues/04-store-requirements.md): Play $25 + 12-tester/14-day closed test; Apple $99/yr + Mac/Xcode; API 36, AAB, 16 KB pages; privacy policy required; no bundled analytics; watch Families-policy risk from cute art.
- [Which 2D engine fits an agent-built pixel-art game for Android + iOS?](issues/01-engine-choice.md): Godot 4.7.x stable with typed GDScript (Defold is the runner-up; Unity and libGDX rejected). CI must not trust Godot's headless exit code.
- [Where can original or licensable pixel art come from, and at what cost?](issues/02-art-sources.md): use only CC0, OGA-BY or commercial licences, plus OFL for fonts (CC-BY conflicts with the App Store). Ninja Adventure (CC0, 16×16) for the world; placeholder Creatures from *50+ Monsters Pack 2D*; final Creatures self-drawn. Keep a licence ledger.
- [What stat, damage and type-chart systems do creature-collector games use?](issues/03-battle-mechanics-reference.md): a menu of genre mechanics with signature mechanics flagged to avoid. Mobile levers: visible randomness, 8–12 types, one energy bar, shown capture %, XP that helps lower levels catch up.
- [What is the core game loop, and what makes Project Wildling different?](issues/05-core-loop-and-pillars.md): for 13+ nostalgic players; Restoration hook (a broken region healed Area by Area, forward-only), backed by Habitat/time of day and visible Bond-shaped growth; 5 ranked pillars; 5–15 min sessions; string-of-pearls Boss order; light postgame.
- [How does a battle work?](issues/07-battle-system.md): 1v1, plus 2v2 in set encounters; 10 Types, no immunities, dual types capped 2×/0.5×; 7 stats (Power/Guard vs Focus/Will); Traits set by where and when caught; one Energy bar + free Basic move; ratio damage with a preview, no random crits; 6 Statuses with countdowns; 3 AI tiers; whole-party XP scaled by level gap; Pokémon-style money loss on defeat, capped at 50%.
- [How do catching, the party, and storage work?](issues/08-catching-and-party.md): visible wild Creatures with per-species behaviour; weighted spawn tables per Area × Restoration stage (Common/Uncommon/Rare/Very rare); Lure offering with a shown 1–95% chance and no fleeing on failure; random starter trio picked by a per-save seed, every starter also wild; Party of 6 with the lead following; unlimited Sanctuary (menu in v1); Collection log doubles as a hunting guide.
- [What should Project Wildling's pixel art look like?](issues/10-art-direction.md): 180 pixels wide internally with integer scaling; 16×16 tiles, player character and Creature map sprites; 64×64 front and back battle sprites animated by code; locked 53-colour palette; Public Pixel and Tiny5 fonts; Restoration shown as tile swaps spreading out from a focal point, muted rather than burnt.
- [How does the game feel on a touchscreen?](issues/11-controls-and-orientation.md): portrait first; floating pad plus tap-to-move; map above a control deck; battle info strip, Area-drawn scene and a thumb-arc command deck; tap to preview, tap again to confirm; 2v2 targeting by tapping a foe sprite; left-handed mirroring; gamepad and keyboard through five input actions.
- [How does Restoration change an Area?](issues/12-restoration.md): Areas start Blighted with 3 stages; stage 1 only from story flags (one may restore several Areas), stages 2+ from K-of-M Mendings (native species caught in the Area, trainers, one Area challenge, hidden sites) that count early; stages change spawns, tiles, patches, NPCs, optional paths, shops, weather, music, never battle rules; focal point shows progress, Region map lists the next stage; save keeps facts plus highest stage, never regresses.
- [How does Bond shape a Creature?](issues/13-bond.md): Bond = a Bond rank (0–5, earned by acting in battles, unlocks 2–4 Bond moves per form) + Leaning needles on four axes (Style, Type, Habitat, Time) the player can steer; evolution at a species level, postponable, the needle picks one of two branches, shown as silhouettes until discovered; no stat effects, no Trait swaps, no Bond items; at most one branch per line in v1.
- [What are the game's title and the in-world name for Creatures?](issues/06-naming-and-branding.md): the title and codename are **Mendvale** (app ID `io.github.andrew689cpu.mendvale`). Creatures are **Verdlings**, players **Menders**; Offering, Haven, Almanac and Mending replace the placeholders. Code keeps generic identifiers; names live only in strings. The store subtitle is "A creature-collecting RPG". The currency and region names are left to other tickets.
- [What is the technical architecture?](issues/09-technical-architecture.md): a pure, seeded core owns every rule (including the overworld grid); scenes only send intent through `GameSession` and play back events. Eight core modules; battles take one turn in and return an event list. JSON content with permanent IDs, baked into one bundle with the linter as the build gate. One-slot JSON save holding only facts, with migrations, saved on OS pause and resumed mid-battle by replay. gdUnit4 with unit, lint and headless-scenario layers. ADRs 0001 and 0002.
- [What items exist, and how does the economy work?](issues/14-items-and-economy.md): one currency, **Marks**, earned once from trainers, challenges, Bosses, visible item spots and selling, never from wild battles or Hidden sites. Daily-regrowing Forage plus a 3-Lure safety net at zero Lures. Care items (percentage heals), 3 Lure tiers plus local Habitat Lures (×2.5, from stage 2), Key items; no held items, boosters or guaranteed catch. One shop per town, stock grows by Bosses beaten and local stage; unlimited 3-pocket bag.
- [How do the Bosses and Area gating sequence the region?](issues/15-bosses-and-gating.md): 5 **Chapters** of 1 / 2 / 3 / 1 / 1 Bosses (ace levels 12 / 22 / 34 / 44 / 52) with Types Wild, Grove + Tide, Stone + Volt (2v2) + Frost, Lumen and a mixed finale; **Blight barriers** cleared by stage 1 are the only gate, and the order comes from reachability, not a list (ADR 0003). Level bands keyed to each Chapter; Boss 1 simulation-tested against every starter; daily postgame rematches at 58–66; challenge-mode level-cap table in data.
- [Which tool authors Area maps, and how do they become content data?](issues/19-map-authoring.md): research recommends **Tiled 1.12 `.tmj`** (CSV tile layers, typed marker classes), baked straight to JSON by `bake_content`, with the YATI importer drawing `TileMapLayer`s; Godot's own `TileMapLayer` scenes are the runner-up (base64 tile data that agents can't diff), and LDtk has stalled. **Adopted** by the human: ADR 0001 and the architecture ticket's Maps section are amended, and the macOS importer check is a new task.
- [How is the Creature roster structured?](issues/16-roster-structure.md): 100 species in 34 lines. 4 Starter trios (T1, T3, T5, T7), all three-stage and branching at the final form, are 48 of them; the other 52 are 6 three-stage, 12 two-stage and 4 single-stage lines (2 rare one-offs). 18 lines branch, and branch forms never spawn wild. Each Type is on 12–20 species. Each line has a debut Chapter, and Chapter 1's two most Common species are a Flame and a Gale line. Level-up moves are shared across a line, there are 6 Stat archetypes, and work goes one line at a time, starters first.
- [How are Mendvale's Android and iOS builds made and released?](issues/17-builds-and-release-pipeline.md): GitHub Actions (free, public repo). `check` on every PR; only the human pushes `v*` tags, which build, sign and upload. Tags set the **Build channel**: `-beta.N` is test, a bare tag is production with no placeholders. One `version_code` formula for both stores. Play internal track via the API, with promotion by hand. iOS is built on `macos-26` and cloud-signed with an API key by our own `xcodebuild`, and the Apple membership waits for the vertical slice. `.godot-version` pin, official templates, fail above 150 MB.
- [How is the region laid out?](issues/18-region-layout.md): one hub town per Chapter, with a spoke to each Boss's Type-themed landmark; 33 Areas (7 towns, 12 routes, 6 dungeons, 8 landmarks) plus 0-stage interiors; 8 Habitat kinds (Meadow, Woodland, Wetland, Coast, Cavern, Crag, Snowfield, Ruins). `starter_chosen` opens the first route, a Boss flag restores its whole spoke, and `chapter_N_open` heals the hub being left and clears the connector's barrier (5 barriers in all). Towns have 1–2 stages and no native species; the linter derives each Area's Chapter; one dormant ferry exit at `harbour` for a second region.
- [Does the YATI importer turn a Tiled Area into Godot tile maps on the Mac, the same way every time?](issues/20-yati-import-check.md): **yes**. YATI 2.2.9 on macOS matches Tiled cell for cell and pixel for pixel, and is deterministic apart from Godot's random node `unique_id`s, so the `set_cell` fallback isn't needed. Import failures exit 0, a missing `.import` sidecar embeds the tileset pixels, and `valid=false` silently stops re-imports, so ticket 09 gains three CI import gates. The map format itself went to [What exactly does an Area's `.tmj` contain?](issues/23-area-map-format.md).
- [How is the move list structured?](issues/21-move-list.md): **144 moves**: 11 level-up moves per Type (a Power and a Focus move at each of 4 tiers, 1 all-foes move, 2 with no damage), 20 Bond-only and 14 signature moves (Starter lines and one-offs, Bond rank 5). An 11-kind closed effect list, at most one side effect per damaging move, no Protect, weather setting or ally-Type synergy. Power and Energy bands from Basic 30/0 to finisher 130/60, with tiers unlocked by level and Bond rank, not power creep. Ticket 07 gains a level term in the damage formula and a flat Energy stat. Card text is generated from the effects.
- [What is Mendvale's story, and which beat sets each flag?](issues/22-story.md): the region is **Mendvale**; the Blight came when the Wellspring's own Steward (Weller, later the Yield's founder and Boss 8) force-tapped it 30 years ago. Bosses are **Stewards** who put their landmark's **root** to sleep until a Mender proves their Bond. Rival Bryn (Gale/Flame, never a Starter) is tempted by the Shade-led Yield and fights beside you in a 2v2, so set encounters gain an AI-controlled ally slot. A full beat sheet for every flag, postgame lines, the one-offs' reasons, a ferry hook, and display names for the 33 Areas and the cast.
- [What exactly does an Area's `.tmj` contain?](issues/23-area-map-format.md): layers `ground` / `detail` / `overhead` / `logic` plus a hidden `stage_N` change set per stage that the shell copies on in rings. A closed logic-kind list (wall, water, counter, 3 ledges, 8 Habitat kinds, erase); Habitat patches are logic tiles that wild Creatures never leave. 15 marker classes with IDs `<area_id>.<name>` and shared stage and flag fields; exits arrive one cell inward. The bake merges class defaults; files are `content/maps/<area_id>.tmj`; a YATI post-processor drops markers and logic from the scene.
- [Assemble the spec](issues/24-assemble-spec.md): the first full [`spec.md`](spec.md), assembled early as current truth from the 23 resolved tickets, with a single 62-rule linter list. It marks the remaining fog as **Open** and found 7 conflicts between tickets, which went to a new ticket. Re-run once the fog clears.
- [Which way do the spec's seven conflicts go?](issues/25-spec-conflicts.md): Snare blocks switching only, and wild fleeing always works; the Collection log shows Rarity per where-found entry; middle forms are wild at Uncommon+ from stage 1 and may be native, final forms are Rare+ from stage 2; only Areas with patches get a payoff Creature; the region record also lists its Areas, unordered; the finale's flag counts for stage 1; OFL is allowed for fonts.
- [What exactly do Google Play and Apple require of Mendvale's accounts and rating?](issues/27-store-facts.md): the Play 12-tester closed test applies (plan on it for every app; internal testers don't count); Apple 13+ for frequent cartoon battles; declare 13+ Play age groups to stay out of Families; an Admin-role API key for iOS cloud signing; declare non-trader on both stores; host the privacy policy and a contact page on GitHub Pages.
- [Where can $0 music and sound effects come from?](issues/26-audio-sources.md): Ninja Adventure's own CC0 audio (40 tracks, 132 SFX) as the base, the same pack as the art; Juhani Junkala's CC0 JRPG packs fill the battle-music gap (check the mix by ear); Kenney, rubberduck and free SubspaceAudio CC0 SFX for footsteps and catch sounds; ChipTone or jsfxr for cries; no AI music. Ogg music re-encoded to about 96 kbps; Restoration crossfades tracks, since no free pack has stems.
- [How is one evolution line designed and reviewed?](issues/28-line-design-method.md): a linted **roster plan** comes first, then one PR per line with a design sheet the human approves. A new `line` record holds the shared moves, Trait slots and spawn entries (ADR 0004). Six archetype share tables, with Energy outside the totals. Weather is clear / rain / fog / snow / storm. 10 battle-only Trait effect kinds, with Trait conditions set by time or weather only. A 10-slot move template, fresh Bond lists, signatures with `category: best`. Trialled on T1 ([prototype branch](https://github.com/andrew689-cpu/rpg/tree/prototype/line-design-t1/prototypes/line-design-t1)).

## Out of scope

- **Online features** (trading, PvP, leaderboards, accounts, servers): offline single-player first; save format should merely not preclude them.
- **Monetisation** (ads, IAP, paid release): hobby/free release.
- **Platforms beyond Android + iOS** (PC, web, consoles).
- **Use of Pokémon IP or ripped assets** in any form.
- **Money sinks** (cosmetics, Sanctuary decor): v1 accepts that late-game money piles up, per [What items exist, and how does the economy work?](issues/14-items-and-economy.md); a fit for a later walkable Sanctuary.
- **Authoring each species line, drawing each Area's map, writing every event and filling the licence ledger:** content work done as implementation slices against the spec, not decisions on this map. The method each line is designed by is decided by [How is one evolution line designed and reviewed?](issues/28-line-design-method.md); the human chose this over keeping all 34 lines on the map (2026-11-14).
