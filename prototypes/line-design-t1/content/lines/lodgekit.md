# Lodgekit line (T1 Tide starter) — PROTOTYPE design sheet

**Plan row:** three-stage starter, trio T1, Tide, Habitat branch (Wetland / Coast) at 34, home Habitat Wetland, Rare wild in Chapter 2.

## Concept
A **beaver**, the builder of wetlands, which fits Mendvale's restoration hook. Its coverage is Grove (it gnaws wood) and Gale (the coastal branch). The Type element is the water it carries: wet fur in streaks, and a tail that throws spray.

| Form | Based on | Silhouette | 2 key colours |

| Lodgekit | beaver kit with a twig | small, round, big flat tail, holds a stick | chestnut brown + river blue |
| Dambrook | adult beaver | broad, tail as wide as its body, water dripping from it | dark chestnut + river blue |
| Mossdam (Wetland) | beaver + its overgrown lodge | a mound: reeds and moss on its back, eyes peering out | moss green + river blue |
| Spraytail (Coast) | beaver gone to sea, sleek like an otter | long, low, rudder tail, surf crest | sea teal + foam white |

## Branch story
**Wetland, Mossdam:** a player who battles in marshes and rivers gets the beaver that becomes part of its dam (Tide/Grove, Bulwark). **Coast, Spraytail:** a player who battles along the coast (Saltmarsh Path, Gullcove and the Tide Shrine in Chapter 2, so before level 34) gets the surf swimmer (Tide/Gale, Skirmisher).

## Do-not-echo check
Not a turtle, frog, otter-with-shell, penguin, seal or mudfish. **Watch:** Spraytail's otter-like sleekness must not drift towards the genre's sea-otter starter. Keep the flat beaver tail and teeth as the key read. **Pass with a note.**
