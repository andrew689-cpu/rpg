## Concept
A **badger** whose white face stripe is an ember. Badgers dig, so the line's coverage is Stone, and its grit is Wild. The Type element is the glowing stripe and the embers along its back.

| Form | Based on | Silhouette | 2 key colours |

| Sootbrock | adult badger, sooty | longer and broader, ember crest along the spine | ash grey + ember orange |
| Forgeback (Power) | badger + anvil and forge | hunched, oversized hammer-flat foreclaws, plated back | iron grey + molten orange |
| Smokesage (Focus) | badger + lantern and smoke | sits upright, long smoke-wisp "beard", pale glowing stripe | smoke lilac + pale gold |

## Branch story
**Power, Forgeback:** a player who keeps hitting with claws gets the smith-badger, which hammers stone (Flame/Stone, Bruiser). **Focus, Smokesage:** a player who uses its sparks and smoke gets the seer, which reads smoke by its den (Flame/Lumen, Caster). The branch is two ways of raising the same animal. Neither is the "evil" one.

### Collection log

- **Brockle:** A badger cub whose white stripe glows like a banked coal. It digs warm dens in meadow banks and naps in them through the heat of the day.
- **Sootbrock:** It tunnels through old charcoal pits, coming up grey with soot. The embers along its back flare when it is cross.
- **Forgeback:** Its claws are hammered hard by digging through hot stone. Folk once followed its trails to find good iron.
- **Smokesage:** It sits very still by its den and reads the smoke. The stripe on its face burns pale, like a lantern turned low.
