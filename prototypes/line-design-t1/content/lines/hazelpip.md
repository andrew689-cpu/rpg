## Concept
A **hazel dormouse**. It's nocturnal (cool palette, large eyes), lives in hedgerows (Grove) and has two real habits: burrowing and hibernating. These give the two branches. The Type element is the briars and seeds that grow from its fur.

| Form | Based on | Silhouette | 2 key colours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hazelpip | dormouse hugging a hazelnut | round ball, huge eyes, curled tail | hazel brown + leaf green |
| Hedgemote | dormouse in a briar nest | bigger ball with a bramble "collar", bushy tail | russet + bramble green |
| Rootdelve (Stone) | dormouse + roots and pebbles | squat and broad, stones grown into its back like a cairn | slate grey + moss green |
| Frostdoze (Frost) | hibernating dormouse + rime | puffed, eyes half-closed, frost-tipped whiskers and tail | ice blue + pine green |

## Branch story
**Stone, Rootdelve:** Stone moves (burrowing) give the dormouse that digs in among the roots and holds the hillside together (Grove/Stone, Bulwark). **Frost, Frostdoze:** Frost moves give the one that sleeps through winter and carries the cold (Grove/Frost, Warden). The base and middle forms learn both coverage Types (Stone at 9, Frost at 17 and 30), so the player can steer either way.

### Collection log

- **Hazelpip:** A dormouse no bigger than a hazelnut, which it often hugs. It is gone at the first loud footstep.
- **Hedgemote:** It weaves a hedge of briars round its nest each autumn. Bramble seeds caught in its fur sprout where it sleeps.
- **Rootdelve:** It burrows down among old roots and wakes with stones grown into its coat. Its dens hold the soil of a whole hillside in place.
- **Frostdoze:** It sleeps through the cold with frost on its whiskers, and the frost does not melt when it wakes. Its breath leaves rime on the leaves.
