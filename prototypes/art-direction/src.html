What should Wildling's pixel art look like?

Two mock screens, drawn the way a phone would draw them: a small internal canvas, scaled up by whole pixels. Flip the options below, or use the presets in the bar at the bottom. On your phone, tap Full screen to see true pixel size, then rotate the phone.

World art: Ninja Adventure (CC0). Battle Creatures: 50+ Monsters Pack 2D (CC0) as placeholders only. Names, moves and UI are invented for this mock.
