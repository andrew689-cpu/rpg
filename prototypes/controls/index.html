Rotate your phone to compare portrait and landscape. Every option below switches live.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Z or Space for A, X or Esc for B, Enter for Menu. A connected gamepad works too (D-pad or left stick, A, B, Start).

Every scheme feeds the same five intents: move(dir), walkTo(tile), confirm, back, menu. The Godot build would map these with InputMap actions, so the choice here is about feel and layout.
