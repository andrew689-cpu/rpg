# Project Wildling

An original 2D pixel-art Creature-collecting RPG for Android and iOS. "Project Wildling" is a placeholder title.

## Language

**Creature**:
A collectible, battle-capable being that the player can catch, raise and add to their team. Placeholder term until an in-world name is chosen.
_Avoid_: Pokémon, monster, mon, pet

### World

**Area**:
A distinct place in the region (a town, route or dungeon) with its own habitats, Creatures and Restoration progress.
_Avoid_: zone, level, map

**Restoration**:
How healed an Area is, which only ever moves forward and is visible in the world itself.
_Avoid_: corruption, purification, cleanse

**Restoration stage**:
One step of an Area's Restoration; the first comes from the story or a boss, later ones from optional local actions.
_Avoid_: tier, phase, level

**Blighted**:
The state an Area starts in before its first Restoration stage: muted, quiet and broken but hopeful.
_Avoid_: corrupted, dead, ruined

**Mending**:
One optional local action that counts toward an Area's next Restoration stage, such as registering a native species, beating the local trainers or challenge, or finding a hidden site. Placeholder name.
_Avoid_: deed, quest, task, objective

**Native species**:
One of the few species an Area lists as belonging there, which counts as registered for that Area when caught inside it.
_Avoid_: local species, regional species

**Area challenge**:
An Area's one optional, stronger set encounter, visible from the start and never on the critical path.
_Avoid_: mini-boss, trial, side boss

**Hidden site**:
An interactable spot in an Area, found by exploring, whose discovery is a Mending.
_Avoid_: secret, collectible, landmark

**Focal point**:
The place in an Area (a spring, a shrine) from which Restoration visibly spreads outward.
_Avoid_: center, origin, heart

**Habitat**:
The kind of terrain, time of day and weather a Creature belongs to, which decides where and when it can be found.
_Avoid_: biome, spawn zone

**Time of day**:
One of morning, day, evening or night on the in-game clock, which advances with play and can be skipped by resting.
_Avoid_: real time, day/night cycle

### Growth

**Bond**:
The visible record of how a Creature has been raised, which steers its evolution branch and the extra moves it learns.
_Avoid_: friendship, affection, happiness, loyalty

**Trait**:
A Creature's visible passive ability, set by the Habitat, Time of day and weather where it was caught; it is never rolled at random.
_Avoid_: ability, nature, personality, IV

### Collecting

**Lure**:
The item a player offers a wild Creature, which it may accept and choose to join them. Placeholder name until naming is decided.
_Avoid_: ball, capture device, trap

**Party**:
The up-to-6 Creatures travelling with the player and available in battle.
_Avoid_: squad, roster

**Sanctuary**:
The place where every Creature not in the Party lives, reachable from any Rest point. Placeholder name.
_Avoid_: PC, box, storage, bank

**Collection log**:
The per-species record of what the player has seen, caught and discovered (where it lives, its Traits, its evolution branches). Placeholder name.
_Avoid_: dex, Pokédex, bestiary

**Starter**:
The first Creature the player picks, from three offered at the start of the game.
_Avoid_: partner, first pick

### Progression

**Boss**:
One of the ~8 major challenges on the critical path; beating one advances the story and a big Restoration stage.
_Avoid_: gym leader, champion, trial captain

**Rest point**:
A camp, inn or town spot where the team heals for free, the in-game clock can be skipped, and the player returns after losing.
_Avoid_: Pokémon Center, save point, checkpoint

### Battle

**Energy**:
A Creature's single pool for paying move costs; it regains a fixed amount each turn and refills after battle.
_Avoid_: PP, stamina, mana, SP

**Basic move**:
The free move every Creature always has, so it can always act even with no Energy.
_Avoid_: Struggle, default attack

**Type**:
One of 10 elemental affinities that a Creature (one or two) and every move have, deciding damage multipliers.
_Avoid_: element, attribute, affinity

**Status**:
A temporary condition with a visible turn countdown; a Creature has at most one, and all clear after battle.
_Avoid_: ailment, condition, debuff

**Set encounter**:
A battle whose format and opponents are fixed in the game's data, such as a trainer, Boss or 2v2 fight, as opposed to a random wild encounter.
_Avoid_: scripted battle, event battle

**Power move**:
A move whose damage pits the user's Power against the target's Guard.
_Avoid_: physical move

**Focus move**:
A move whose damage pits the user's Focus against the target's Will.
_Avoid_: special move, magic move

**Rarity tier**:
How often a species spawns in a given place: Common, Uncommon, Rare or Very rare.
_Avoid_: spawn rate, encounter rate

**Habitat patch**:
A marked stretch of ground inside an Area (tall grass, shallows, a cave floor) where wild Creatures of matching Habitats wander.
_Avoid_: grass, encounter zone, spawn point
