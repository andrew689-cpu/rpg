# Mendvale

An original 2D pixel-art Creature-collecting RPG for Android and iOS. "Mendvale" is both the title and the internal codename.

## Language

**Creature**:
A collectible, battle-capable being that the player can catch, raise and add to their team.
In-world name: **Verdling** (plural Verdlings).
_Avoid_: Pokémon, monster, mon, pet

### World

**Region**:
The whole connected world of Areas the player restores; the Medium game has one, itself called Mendvale.
_Avoid_: world, map, continent

**Blight**:
The quiet withering that crept down the vale from the soured Wellspring, leaving Areas and their Creatures muted.
_Avoid_: corruption, plague, darkness

**Wellspring**:
The spring at the region's heart that once fed every root, and where the Blight began.
_Avoid_: fountain, core, heart

**Root**:
A landmark's focal point: a spring once fed by the Wellspring, put to sleep by its Boss to keep it from the Blight until a Trainer proves able to wake it.
_Avoid_: shrine, seal, node

**Area**:
A distinct place in the region (a town, route, dungeon, landmark or interior) with its own habitats, Creatures and Restoration progress.
_Avoid_: zone, level, map

**Hub town**:
The town at the start of a Chapter, from which one spoke leads to each of that Chapter's Bosses and a barred route leads on to the next Chapter.
_Avoid_: central town, crossroads

**Spoke**:
The short chain of Areas leading from a hub town to one Boss's landmark.
_Avoid_: branch, path, arm

**Landmark**:
A small, special Area at the end of a spoke where a Boss (or a rare one-off) waits, shaped by its Type.
_Avoid_: arena, gym, boss room

**Interior**:
An indoor Area (a house, shop or inn) that belongs to an outdoor parent Area and is never Blighted.
_Avoid_: room, building, sub-area

**Restoration**:
How healed an Area is, which only ever moves forward and is visible in the world itself.
_Avoid_: corruption, purification, cleanse

**Restoration stage**:
One step of an Area's Restoration; the first comes from the story or a boss, later ones from optional local actions.
_Avoid_: tier, phase, level

**Blighted**:
The state an Area starts in before its first Restoration stage: muted, quiet and broken but hopeful.
_Avoid_: corrupted, dead, ruined

**Mending**:
One optional local action that counts toward an Area's next Restoration stage, such as registering a native species, beating the local trainers or challenge, or finding a hidden site.
_Avoid_: deed, quest, task, objective

**Native species**:
One of the few species an Area lists as belonging there, which counts as registered for that Area when caught inside it.
_Avoid_: local species, regional species

**Area challenge**:
An Area's one optional, stronger set encounter, visible from the start and never on the critical path.
_Avoid_: mini-boss, trial, side boss

**Hidden site**:
An interactable spot in an Area, found by exploring, whose discovery is a Mending.
_Avoid_: secret, collectible, landmark

**Focal point**:
The place in an Area (a spring, a shrine) from which Restoration visibly spreads outward.
_Avoid_: center, origin, heart

**Habitat**:
The kind of terrain, time of day and weather a Creature belongs to, which decides where and when it can be found.
_Avoid_: biome, spawn zone

**Habitat kind**:
One of the named terrain kinds (Meadow, Woodland, Wetland, Coast, Cavern, Crag, Snowfield, Ruins); every species has exactly one as its home.
_Avoid_: biome, terrain type, environment

**Time of day**:
One of morning, day, evening or night on the in-game clock, which advances with play and can be skipped by resting.
_Avoid_: real time, day/night cycle

### Growth

**Bond**:
The visible record of how a Creature has been raised, made of its Bond rank and its Leanings; it steers the evolution branch and the extra moves it learns, and never its stats.
_Avoid_: friendship, affection, happiness, loyalty

**Bond rank**:
How much a Creature has been raised: a level that only grows, earned by acting in battles.
_Avoid_: friendship level, affection, grade, potential

**Leaning**:
Which way a Creature has been raised along one axis (style, type, habitat or time), a needle the player can steer back and forth; it picks between two evolution branches.
_Avoid_: alignment, personality, nature, bias

**Evolution line**:
A base species together with every species it can evolve into; each form in a line is its own species.
_Avoid_: family, evolution tree, chain

**Branch form**:
One of the two species a branching species can evolve into, picked by its Leaning.
_Avoid_: variant, split evolution, alternate form

**Bond move**:
A move a Creature learns by reaching a Bond rank rather than a level; each form has its own short list of them.
_Avoid_: friendship move, egg move, tutor move

**Signature move**:
A move that belongs to exactly one evolution line, learned only at the highest Bond rank.
_Avoid_: exclusive move, unique move, Z-move

**Stat archetype**:
A species' broad battle role (such as Bruiser or Bulwark), which shapes how its stats are spread.
_Avoid_: class, role, build

**Trait**:
A Creature's visible passive ability, set by the Habitat, Time of day and weather where it was caught; it is never rolled at random.
_Avoid_: ability, nature, personality, IV

### Collecting

**Trainer**:
Anyone who raises and battles with Creatures, the player included.
In-world name: **Mender**.
_Avoid_: tamer, keeper, ranger

**Lure**:
The item a player offers a wild Creature, which it may accept and choose to join them.
In-world name: **Offering**.
_Avoid_: ball, capture device, trap

**Party**:
The up-to-6 Creatures travelling with the player and available in battle.
_Avoid_: squad, roster

**Sanctuary**:
The place where every Creature not in the Party lives, reachable from any Rest point.
In-world name: **Haven**.
_Avoid_: PC, box, storage, bank

**Collection log**:
The per-species record of what the player has seen, caught and discovered (where it lives, its Traits, its evolution branches).
In-world name: **Almanac**.
_Avoid_: dex, Pokédex, bestiary

**Starter**:
The first Creature the player picks, from three offered at the start of the game.
_Avoid_: partner, first pick

**Starter trio**:
The three Starters offered together, whose Types form a triangle where each beats the next.
_Avoid_: starter set, starter group

### Items and money

**Money**:
The game's single currency, earned from trainers, Bosses, finds and selling, and spent in shops.
In-world name: **Marks**.
_Avoid_: gold, coins, cash, Poké Dollars

**Forage**:
A Habitat-specific pickup (an herb, a berry) that grows back each in-game day in a fitting spot.
_Avoid_: berry tree, harvest, drop

**Habitat Lure**:
A Lure that works much better on Creatures of one Habitat, sold where that Habitat lives.
_Avoid_: specialty ball, net ball

### Progression

**Boss**:
One of the ~8 major challenges on the critical path; beating one advances the story and a big Restoration stage.
In-world name: **Steward** (plural Stewards).
_Avoid_: gym leader, champion, trial captain

**Chapter**:
A group of Bosses on offer together, with the Areas that lead to them; the next Chapter opens once every Boss in the current one is beaten.
_Avoid_: tier, act, stage, gym circuit

**Blight barrier**:
An obstacle on a route, such as brambles, a dried ford or a rockfall, that only an Area's first Restoration stage clears.
_Avoid_: roadblock, gate, HM obstacle

**Rest point**:
A camp, inn or town spot where the team heals for free, the in-game clock can be skipped, and the player returns after losing.
_Avoid_: Pokémon Center, save point, checkpoint

### Battle

**Energy**:
A Creature's single pool for paying move costs; it regains a fixed amount each turn and refills after battle.
_Avoid_: PP, stamina, mana, SP

**Basic move**:
The free move every Creature always has, so it can always act even with no Energy.
_Avoid_: Struggle, default attack

**Type**:
One of 10 elemental affinities that a Creature (one or two) and every move have, deciding damage multipliers.
_Avoid_: element, attribute, affinity

**Status**:
A temporary condition with a visible turn countdown; a Creature has at most one, and all clear after battle.
_Avoid_: ailment, condition, debuff

**Set encounter**:
A battle whose format and opponents are fixed in the game's data, such as a trainer, Boss or 2v2 fight, as opposed to a random wild encounter.
_Avoid_: scripted battle, event battle

**Power move**:
A move whose damage pits the user's Power against the target's Guard.
_Avoid_: physical move

**Focus move**:
A move whose damage pits the user's Focus against the target's Will.
_Avoid_: special move, magic move

**Rarity tier**:
How often a species spawns in a given place: Common, Uncommon, Rare or Very rare.
_Avoid_: spawn rate, encounter rate

**Habitat patch**:
A marked stretch of ground inside an Area (tall grass, shallows, a cave floor) where wild Creatures of matching Habitats wander.
_Avoid_: grass, encounter zone, spawn point

### Story

**Faction**:
The misguided group that soured the Wellspring by forcing Creatures to grow, and still means to finish the job.
In-world name: **the Yield** (members: Yield hands).
_Avoid_: team, gang, villains, cult

**Rival**:
The player's friend from the home town who sets out the same morning and is met in battle at each new Chapter.
_Avoid_: nemesis, opponent, competitor

**Mentor**:
The retired Trainer in the home town who sends the player north and whose old field notebook becomes the Collection log.
_Avoid_: professor, guide, elder

**Lantern-keeper**:
The person who tends a Rest point and keeps spare Lures for a player who has run out.
_Avoid_: nurse, attendant, caretaker

### Releases

**Build channel**:
What a build is for, which sets what it may contain: debug (has the debug menu and fixture saves), test (a release build that may still use placeholder Creature art) or production (store-ready, no placeholders).
_Avoid_: build type, flavour, environment

**Vertical slice**:
The first playable cut of the game: Starter trio T1 and the Chapter 1 lines, with their Areas, up to Boss 1.
_Avoid_: demo, MVP, alpha
